JobAssist — find jobs that fit your CV
========================================

JobAssist searches job sites, employers' job boards and company websites for a job
title you choose, then uses Claude to score every job against your CV and explain why
it fits. It runs on your own computer; your CV and results stay on it.


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
- A Mac (macOS 12 or later) or a Windows 10/11 PC, with internet.
- An Anthropic API key, from https://console.anthropic.com (add a few dollars of credit).
  JobAssist shows an estimate before each search, and stops at a spending limit
  ($5 per search, $2 for starred-only searches, at most $0.20 per employer).
- Optional, free: Adzuna keys (https://developer.adzuna.com) and a Reed key
  (https://www.reed.co.uk/developers) for many more job listings.


START IT
--------
1. Unzip the download and keep everything in the JobAssist folder together.

2. Windows: double-click  "JobAssist for Windows.exe"
   Mac:     double-click  "JobAssist for Mac"

   The first time, your computer may warn you because JobAssist isn't from an app store:
   - Windows: on "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   - Mac: if it says it can't be opened, right-click (or Control-click) "JobAssist for Mac",
     choose "Open", then "Open" again. Or: System Settings > Privacy & Security >
     "Open Anyway".

3. The first time only, a window asks for your API keys and then spends a few
   minutes installing what JobAssist needs (a small tool called uv, the app's
   components, and a browser used to read company websites).

4. Your web browser opens JobAssist at  http://localhost:8000
   Keep the JobAssist window open while you use it. Close it to stop JobAssist.

Next time it starts in a few seconds. If you double-click it while it's already
running, it just opens the page again.


USING IT
--------
- Add your CV (PDF, Word or text) — it's remembered.
- Type a job title. Similar titles appear underneath; untick any you don't want.
- Choose where to look: your Excel list of employers, or all job sites.
  No list yet? Use "Make an employer list" at the bottom of the page — type one
  company per line (see "Example employer list.txt") and download the spreadsheet.
- Press "Find jobs". "Stop searching" moves straight on to scoring what was found.
- Results are ranked by match; press "Why?" for the reasoning, or download them.


WHERE THINGS ARE KEPT
---------------------
- Your API keys:        the .env file in this folder (keep it private; don't share it)
- Saved results/scores: a "jobassist" folder in your home folder (.jobassist)
- Logs, if something goes wrong: .jobassist/logs in your home folder

To uninstall, delete this folder and the .jobassist folder in your home folder.
